# CRM Module: Deals

The **Deals** section is where sales opportunities are tracked and managed as they move through the sales pipeline.

## Key Features

### 1. Pipeline View
- **Purpose**: To provide a visual, Kanban-style overview of the sales pipeline.
- **Functionality**: Deals are represented as cards that can be dragged and dropped between columns representing different stages of the sales process (e.g., "Lead In," "Proposal Sent," "Won"). Each column displays the total number of deals and their combined value.

### 2. List View
- **Purpose**: To offer a detailed, tabular view of all deals.
- **Functionality**: A searchable and sortable table that allows for easy filtering of deals by stage, owner, value, or close date. This view is ideal for reporting and bulk actions.

### 3. Forecasting
- **Purpose**: To provide AI-powered sales projections.
- **Functionality**: This section uses historical data and current pipeline status to generate revenue forecasts. It displays key metrics like Projected Revenue, Pipeline Coverage, and Quota Attainment to help with strategic planning.
