# CRM Module: Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the command center for the CRM module, providing users with a high-level overview of sales activities, performance metrics, and personal tasks. It is composed of several L3 sections accessible via a dedicated sidebar.

## Key Features

### 1. Overview
- **Purpose**: To provide a snapshot of key sales metrics and recent activities.
- **Functionality**: Features KPI (Key Performance Indicator) cards for metrics like New Leads, Deals Won, and Pipeline Value. It also includes a sales funnel visualization and a live feed of recent CRM activities.

### 2. Team Performance
- **Purpose**: To visualize the performance of the sales team.
- **Functionality**: Displays AI-generated charts comparing team members on metrics such as Deals Closed and Revenue Generated, allowing managers to quickly assess team productivity.

### 3. My Tasks
- **Purpose**: A personal task management board for CRM-related activities.
- **Functionality**: A Kanban-style board with columns for "To Do," "In Progress," and "Completed." Users can add, manage, and track their follow-ups, proposals, and other sales tasks.
