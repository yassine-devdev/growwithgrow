# CRM Module: Analytics

The **Analytics** section provides powerful reporting and data visualization tools to help users understand sales performance, track activities, and identify key trends.

## Key Features

### 1. Sales Reports
- **Purpose**: To offer a comprehensive overview of sales performance.
- **Functionality**: Features a dashboard with key performance indicators (KPIs) and AI-generated charts that visualize sales revenue, deal win/loss ratios, and sales cycle length.

### 2. Activity Reports
- **Purpose**: To track and analyze the volume and type of sales activities.
- **Functionality**: Displays charts breaking down activities by type (emails, calls, meetings) and showing activity trends over time. This helps managers assess team productivity and effort.

### 3. Lead Sources
- **Purpose**: To measure the effectiveness of different lead generation channels.
- **Functionality**: Provides charts that compare lead sources based on the number of leads generated and their conversion rates, helping to optimize marketing spend and strategy.
