# Knowledge Base: Curriculum

The **Curriculum** section is the core of the Knowledge Base, designed for managing and navigating educational content and standards.

## Key Features

### 1. Browse
- **Purpose**: To provide a structured interface for exploring the entire curriculum.
- **Functionality**: Users can navigate through subjects, grade levels, and units to find specific lesson plans, resources, and learning objectives.

### 2. Standards Alignment
- **Purpose**: To map curriculum content to educational standards (e.g., Common Core, NGSS).
- **Functionality**: A tool that allows educators and administrators to see how individual pieces of content align with various standards. It helps ensure that the curriculum meets required educational guidelines.

### 3. Version History
- **Purpose**: To track changes and updates made to curriculum content over time.
- **Functionality**: Provides a complete history for any piece of content, allowing users to view past versions, compare changes, and revert to an older version if necessary. This is crucial for maintaining curriculum integrity and auditing changes.
