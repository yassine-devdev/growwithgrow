# Knowledge Base: Store

The **Store** is a digital marketplace within the Knowledge Base for purchasing educational materials and resources. It uses a multi-level navigation system to organize its offerings.

## Navigation and Features

The store is divided into three main L3 categories: Books, Courses, and Exams. Each category has its own set of L4 tools for browsing and interaction.

### 1. Books
- **Search & Filter**: Find books by title, author, or subject.
- **Categories**: Browse books by genre or educational category.
- **View Details**: See detailed information about a book before purchasing.

### 2. Courses
- **Search & Filter**: Find courses based on topic, difficulty, or instructor.
- **View Syllabus**: Review the curriculum and lesson plan for a course.
- **Enroll**: Purchase and enroll in online courses.

### 3. Exams
- **Practice Tests**: Access practice materials to prepare for exams.
- **Timed Exams**: Purchase and take official timed exams.
- **Results**: View and analyze performance on past exams.
