# Communications Module: Email

The **Email** section is a fully integrated email client designed for efficient communication within the application's ecosystem. It combines standard email functionality with powerful AI-driven tools to enhance productivity.

## Layout
The Email client features a two-panel layout:
- **L3 Sidebar**: A vertical navigation bar on the left provides access to standard mail folders and innovative AI tools.
- **Main Content Area**: Displays the list of messages or the content of a selected email.

## Standard Features
The L3 sidebar provides access to all standard email folders:
- **Inbox**: For incoming mail.
- **Sent**: A record of all sent messages.
- **Drafts**: For saved, unsent messages.
- **Spam**: Houses messages flagged as junk.
- **Archive**: For storing emails that don't need to be in the main inbox.

## AI Innovation Tools
A dedicated section in the sidebar offers unique, AI-powered features:
- **Smart Triage (AI)**: Automatically categorizes and prioritizes incoming emails based on content and sender, helping users focus on what's important.
- **AI Scribe**: An AI assistant that helps draft replies, summarize long email threads, and suggest action items.
- **Contact Hub**: An intelligent contact management system that automatically builds profiles for contacts based on email interactions.
- **Thread Weaver**: An AI tool that visualizes complex email conversations, making it easy to follow long and convoluted threads.
