# Communications Module: Calendar

The **Calendar** section is an integrated scheduling tool that helps users manage their time, coordinate with team members, and handle bookings seamlessly.

## Key Features

### 1. My Calendar
- A personal calendar view where users can see their own appointments, events, and deadlines.
- It allows for the creation of new events, setting reminders, and managing personal availability.

### 2. Team Schedule
- A shared view that overlays the schedules of team members, making it easy to find suitable meeting times and coordinate group activities.
- Permissions can be configured to respect privacy while facilitating collaboration.

### 3. Bookings
- A feature that allows users to create public booking pages.
- External parties or other team members can use these pages to schedule meetings directly on the user's calendar based on their available time slots, eliminating the back-and-forth of scheduling.
